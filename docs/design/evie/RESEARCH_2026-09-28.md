| Ovia | Everything; invitees can re-share | Avoid |

**Adopt:** "What your partner sees" preview (decrypts the outgoing payload
locally) · "share from date" limit · one-way only · revoke copy says "stops
future updates".

## 4. E2EE relay design (recommended)

- **Crypto:** WebCrypto X25519 + HKDF + AES-256-GCM. Supported in Android
  WebView (Chrome 133+) and iOS **18.4+**
  ([Chrome](https://chromestatus.com/feature/6291245926973440),
  [WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/16574/webkit-features-in-safari-18-4/)).
  Fallback: `@noble/curves` (MIT, audited).
- **Pairing:** QR carries a 256-bit secret (the in-person channel is the
  authentication). 4-word phrase = HKDF(transcript) → EFF short wordlist (~41
  bits). The 6-char code is only a mailbox lookup, never a secret.
- **Relay:** one Cloudflare Durable Object per mailbox, one padded blob
  (≤64 KB), monotonic version checked by compare-and-swap, write/read tokens,
  logs off, idle mailboxes self-delete (~60 days). Free tier is ample.
- **Leaks we can't remove:** IP, timing, size. Mitigate with fixed-size
  padding and a steady daily upload plus jittered change uploads.
- **Revoke:** DELETE wipes the mailbox; re-pair makes new keys and mailbox.
