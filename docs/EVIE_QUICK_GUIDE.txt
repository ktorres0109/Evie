PART 2 — FOR JASMINE (show her this)
------------------------------------
What it is
  Evie is a private period and cycle tracker, in Spanish and English.

Private by design
  - Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI.
  - Kelvin can't see anything. If you ever want, you can share a little with him.
    You pick what, and you can stop anytime.

How to use it
  - Setup: if you remember when your last 2-3 periods started, enter them.
    Then you get an estimate right away. Rough dates are fine.
  - Every day (or whenever): tap + and log what you want: flow, symptoms, mood.
    Hold a symptom to say how strong it was. Skipping days is fine.
  - Next period: you get a range of days, not one fake-exact date.
    "Why this estimate?" shows how it got there.
  - Birth control: add it under You -> Birth control for pack-day info and reminders.
  - Something unusual affecting your cycle? You -> Things that affect your cycle.
    It stays private and is never shared.
  - Lost? You -> How Evie works (a 2-minute guide).

Good to know
  - It gives estimates, not diagnoses, and it is NOT birth control.
  - Don't uninstall it. Make a backup now and then:
    You -> Privacy & data -> Export everything.
  - It's a test version. Tell Kelvin anything that's confusing, broken, or annoying.
